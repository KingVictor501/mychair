# Contact and Support

This page tells you who to contact for what. The contact details below are for the sample app.

## Who to ask first

For anything about a specific appointment, service, price, or deposit, contact the shop first. The shop's page shows its phone number. Only the shop can change its prices or honor a deposit, and the reserved pro or the owner can remove a no-show strike.

## MyChair support

For problems with the app itself, such as you cannot log in, a page will not load, or you think your account was used by someone else, contact MyChair support. Email admin@mychair.com. Support usually replies within one business day.

## What to include in a support request

Include your name, the shop and pro involved, the date and time of the appointment, and what you saw. Do not send your password or any card numbers. Support will never ask for your password.

## Report a privacy problem

If you think someone saw information they should not have, or you want your information corrected or deleted, email admin@mychair.com. Say which shop it involves.

## Report a safety or abuse problem

If a person at a shop treated you badly or is misusing the app, tell the shop's owner. If the owner is the problem, or you cannot reach them, email admin@mychair.com.

## Accessibility

If a page is hard to use with a keyboard or screen reader, tell support the page and the problem. We want every page to be usable and will fix what we find.

## Help inside the app

Most questions are answered in the help articles. You can ask the help assistant a question; it answers only from MyChair's own documents and says so when it does not know.
