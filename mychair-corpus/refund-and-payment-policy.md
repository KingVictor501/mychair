# MyChair Refund and Payment Policy

MyChair is a booking and client-management app. It does not take real payments. This page explains what that means for paying, deposits, and refunds.

## Paying for a service

You pay the shop directly, in person, the way you always have. MyChair does not charge your card, store card numbers, or move money. The price you see when you book is the price the shop has set for that pro and service.

## What the payment status means

Each appointment carries a payment status that the pro or owner records. It is a note, not a transaction. The three statuses are Paid (the shop was paid), Deposit held (the shop is holding a deposit the client handed over), and Cash (the client paid in cash). Clients can see the status on their own appointments.

## Deposits

A shop may ask for a deposit on certain services. Any deposit is handled between you and the shop outside MyChair. When the shop receives it, they mark the appointment as Deposit held.

## Refunds

Because MyChair does not process payments, MyChair cannot issue refunds. If you were charged for a service you did not receive, or you are unhappy with a service, talk to the shop. Refunds are decided by the shop's owner under that shop's own rules.

## Cancelled appointments and deposits

If you cancel outside the cancellation window, ask the shop whether your deposit is returned. If you cancel inside the window or are marked a no-show, the shop decides whether a deposit is kept. See the cancellation and no-show policy for the windows.

## Price changes

Only the owner (or the Super Admin) can change prices. A price change applies to appointments booked after the change. The price shown when you booked is the price on your appointment.

## Disputes about a charge

If you think a shop took money it should not have, contact the shop first. If you cannot reach them, contact MyChair support. MyChair can look at the appointment record, but cannot reverse a payment it never handled.
