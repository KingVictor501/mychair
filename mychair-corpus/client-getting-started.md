# Getting Started as a Client

This guide walks a new client from sign-up to a booked appointment.

## Create your account

Choose Sign up and enter your name and phone number, an optional email, and a password. Sign-up is open to anyone. If a pro sent you an invite link, open that link first. It sets you up as that pro's client right away.

## Find a shop and a pro

After signing in you see the shops on your account and a list of their pros. Each pro shows their role (for example barber, nail tech, or lash tech), their live status, their services, and starting prices. Open a pro's page to see more.

## Search for a service by meaning

The search box finds services by what you mean, not only the exact words. Searching "lashes" finds eyelash extensions and lash fills, and also pros whose main job is something else but who offer lashes, such as a nail tech. Searching "something for a fuller look" can surface the same service.

## Pick a time

Choose a service and a pro. MyChair shows the open times in the shop's time zone, inside that pro's booking window (5 days ahead by default). Times that are taken, on a break, or on a day off do not appear.

## Confirm your booking

Check the pro, service, time, length, and price, then confirm. The length uses your pro's saved time estimate for you if there is one. Your appointment appears on the Appointments page with the status Waiting.

## See, move, or cancel

The Appointments page lists your upcoming and past bookings. You can reschedule or cancel up to 2 hours before the start by default. See the cancellation and no-show policy.

## What you will not see

You cannot see other clients, a pro's pay structure, or another shop's private information.
