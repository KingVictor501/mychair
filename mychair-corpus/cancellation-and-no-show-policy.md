# MyChair Cancellation and No-Show Policy

This policy covers changing or cancelling an appointment and what happens when a client does not show up. These are the MyChair defaults. An owner can change the cancellation window for their shop, and the shop page shows the number that applies.

## How to cancel or reschedule

Open the Appointments page, pick the appointment, and choose Reschedule or Cancel. You can only change your own appointments. Rescheduling moves your booking to a new open time with the same pro. Cancelling frees the time for other clients and sets the appointment's status to Cancelled.

## The cancellation window

By default you can cancel or reschedule on your own up to 2 hours before the appointment starts. Inside that window the Cancel button is turned off. To change an appointment that is inside the window, contact the shop directly. The pro, the owner, or the Super Admin can still cancel it for you.

## What counts as a no-show

A no-show is when a client does not arrive for a booked appointment and did not cancel. The pro (or the owner) marks the appointment as No-show. Only a waiting appointment can be marked a no-show. A cancelled or completed appointment never counts as one.

## No-show strikes

Each no-show adds one strike to that client's record at that shop. Strikes are counted per shop, so a missed appointment at one shop does not count against you at another. A pro can see how many strikes their own clients have. The owner can see the strikes for every client in the shop, and so can the Super Admin. Other clients cannot see them.

## Strikes do not block booking

A strike is a record, not a penalty built into the app. MyChair does not stop a client from booking after a no-show. How a pro or shop responds to repeated no-shows is up to them. Cancelling on time never adds a strike.

## If something went wrong

If you were marked a no-show by mistake, for example the pro was running late, the pro who was reserved for that appointment can remove the strike themselves. The owner and the Super Admin can also see and remove strikes. If you think a strike is wrong, ask your pro or contact the shop.
