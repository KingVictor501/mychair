# Standard and Custom Pricing

Prices on MyChair are set by the shop, not by the app and not by individual pros. This page explains the two ways a pro's price can be set.

## The standard price list

Each shop has one standard price list: a price for each service. Most pros use it. When a pro uses the standard list, their prices match the shop's list exactly, and a change to the shop's price changes theirs.

## Custom prices

A busy or specialized pro may charge more. The owner can set custom prices for that pro, one price per service. A custom price replaces the standard price for that pro only. Other pros are unaffected.

## Turning Standard price back on

The owner can switch a pro back to Standard price at any time. The pro's prices then follow the shop's list again. Their custom prices are kept in case the owner turns them back on.

## Who can change prices

Only the owner and the Super Admin can set standard prices, set custom prices, or turn Standard price on or off. Pros cannot change prices, including their own.

## What clients see

Clients see the price that applies to the pro they chose, on the pro's page and before confirming a booking. A client comparing two pros at the same shop may see different prices for the same service if one pro has custom prices. Pro pages list a starting price per service.

## When a price changes

A new price applies to appointments booked after the change. An appointment already booked keeps the price shown when it was booked.

## Prices differ between shops

Each shop sets its own prices. The same service may cost different amounts at two shops.
