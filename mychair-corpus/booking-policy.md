# MyChair Booking Policy

This policy explains how booking works on MyChair. MyChair is an app that many independent shops use (barbershops, salons, nail shops, lash studios, and similar). The rules below are the MyChair defaults. A shop's owner can change some of them for their own shop, and the shop's page shows the numbers that apply there.

## Who can book

Anyone with a MyChair client account can book. Signing up needs your name and phone number. An email address is optional. You book for yourself only. A pro or an owner cannot book an appointment on behalf of a client through the client's account.

## How far ahead you can book

By default you can book up to 5 days ahead with any pro. A pro who is in high demand can extend their own booking window, for example to 14 days, and that pro's page will show the longer window. You cannot book in the past, and you cannot book a time the pro has marked as a break or a day off.

## How the length of your appointment is set

Each service has a default length, such as 30 minutes for a haircut. If your pro has saved a time estimate on your style card for the service you usually get, MyChair uses that estimate instead, so the booked slot matches how long your service really takes. You will see the length before you confirm.

## No double booking

A time slot can only be booked once. If two people try to book the same open time, the first confirmed booking wins and the second person is asked to pick another time. This is enforced on the server, not just on the screen.

## Times and time zones

Every time you see in MyChair is shown in the shop's own time zone, which is set by the shop. If you are traveling, an appointment still shows the shop's local time.

## Reminders

MyChair does not send text or email reminders at this time. Please note the date and time when you book, or check the Appointments page.
