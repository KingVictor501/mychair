You must give a real name and a working phone number. You are responsible for keeping your password private and for what happens under your account. Do not share your login. If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and contact support.

## Roles

An account has one role: client, pro, or owner. Clients can sign up on their own. Pros join a shop through an invite link from the owner or an account the owner created for them. Only the owner or the Super Admin can change a pro's role. Pros cannot change their own role or any prices.

## What you agree to do

## What shops agree to do

Shops and pros agree to honor the prices and times shown, keep client information private, and use client information only to provide their services. Shops set their own cancellation windows, deposits, and refund decisions.
