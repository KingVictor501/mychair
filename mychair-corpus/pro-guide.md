# Guide for Pros

This guide is for barbers, stylists, nail techs, lash techs, and other pros who work at a shop on MyChair.

## Joining a shop

You join in one of two ways: through a pro invite link from your shop's owner, or from an account the owner created for you. With an invite link you choose your role from the roles the shop has turned on. With an owner-created account you get a one-time setup link and choose your own password. The owner never sees it. After you join, only the owner or the Super Admin can change your role.

## Set your hours and breaks

Open your schedule and set weekly working hours, breaks, and days off. Open times for clients are made from these rules and exclude anything already booked. Times are in the shop's time zone.

## Set your live status

Set your status to Available, With a client, or On break. Clients see it on your page.

## List your services

Add the services you offer, with a name, description, and length. You can list services outside your main role. A nail tech who also does lashes can list lash services and will appear when clients search for lashes. You cannot change prices; only the owner can.

## Your booking window

Clients can book 5 days ahead by default. You can extend your own window if you want more notice, for example 14 days.

## Your cancellation and no-show policy

Your owner decides whether you can set your own cancellation window. If they allow it, you can change the window for your own appointments, and if they allow that too, you can choose whether your policy is also shown on your profile page. If they have not allowed it, you follow the shop's window. Clients always see the window that applies to you when they confirm a booking, whatever you choose. You can remove a no-show strike for an appointment you were reserved for, for example when you were running late.

## Your clients

You see only your own clients, and only their name, phone number, and style card. You never see their email or another pro's clients.

## Working through an appointment

You move an appointment from Waiting to In chair to Completed, or mark it No-show or Cancelled. You can also record the payment status: Paid, Deposit held, or Cash. Completed appointments are added to the client's visit history on their style card.

## Sending a client invite

Create a client invite link and send it from your own phone. See the client invite links article.
