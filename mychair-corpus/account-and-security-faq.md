# Account and Security FAQ

Quick answers about logging in and keeping your account safe.

## How do I reset a forgotten password?

Password reset by email is not available yet. Contact support at admin@mychair.com and they will help you recover access after confirming who you are. Support never asks for your current password.

## Why does it say "Invalid email or password"?

The message is the same whether the email or the password is wrong, so no one can use it to find out which accounts exist. Check both and try again.

## Why does it say "Too many attempts, try again in a few minutes"?

To stop guessing, MyChair limits how often you can log in, sign up, book, or search. Wait a few minutes and try again. The limit resets by itself.

## How are passwords protected?

Passwords are stored only as a one-way scrambled value (a hash), so nobody, including MyChair, can read your password. Sessions use a signed token that expires, so you will need to log in again after a while.

## What if I am a pro whose owner created my account?

The owner gives you a one-time setup link. Open it and choose your own password. The owner never sees it. If the link has expired, ask the owner to send a new one.

## Can I use MyChair on my phone?

Yes. The website works on phones, tablets, and computers. Owners should use the website for shop setup.

## Can the app owner see my account?

The Super Admin can see data across shops to run and support the app. When the Super Admin views the app as another user, that session is logged.

## What should I do if I think my account was used by someone else?

Change your password right away and email admin@mychair.com with the shop involved.
