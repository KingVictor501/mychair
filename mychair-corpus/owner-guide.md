# Guide for Shop Owners

This guide is for the person who runs a shop on MyChair.

## Set up your shop

Pick your business type: barbershop, salon, nail shop, lash studio, or multi-service. One click sets up the matching pro roles and a starter list of services. You can turn roles on or off later. Then set your shop name, time zone, booking window, and cancellation window.

## Cancellation and no-show settings

You set the shop's cancellation window. You can also turn on two options: let pros set their own cancellation window, and let pros choose whether their policy is displayed on their profile page. Both are off until you turn them on. Whatever you choose, clients always see the window that applies before they confirm a booking. You can see every pro's window and every no-show strike in your shop.

## Bring in your team

You have two options. Send a pro invite link, and the pro signs up and picks a role you have turned on. Or create the account yourself by entering the pro's name, email, and role. The pro then gets a one-time setup link and chooses their own password. You never see or set a pro's password.

## Roles

You and the Super Admin are the only people who can change a pro's role. A pro cannot change their own.

## Prices

You and the Super Admin are the only people who set prices. Set the shop's standard price list. For each pro, either turn on Standard price or set custom prices. Pros cannot change prices. See the standard and custom pricing article.

## Station and pay structure

Set each pro's station number and pay structure: chair rent or commission percentage. Pay structure is visible only to you and the Super Admin. Pros and clients never see it.

## Seeing your shop

You can see every pro's schedule and every appointment in your shop. You can change an appointment's status, cancel or reschedule it, and set its payment status. You can see a client's name, phone, email, and the no-show count.

## Style card sharing

Each pro keeps their own style card for a client. If two pros need to share a card, ask the owner. You can grant or remove sharing between two pros.

## Working as a pro

If you also cut hair or do nails, you can take appointments yourself and switch to your own pro view.
