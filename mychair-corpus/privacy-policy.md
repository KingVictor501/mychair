# MyChair Privacy Policy

This page explains what MyChair collects, who can see it, and how it is protected. MyChair is built so that one shop never sees another shop's data, and a pro never sees another pro's clients.

## What we collect from clients

A client account has a name, a phone number, and optionally an email address. When you book, we record the appointment: the shop, the pro, the service, the time, and its status and payment status. Your pro may also keep a style card about you (see below).

## What we collect from shops

Owners and pros give their name, email, role, and shop details. Pay structure (chair rent or commission percentage) is collected for the owner's records and is visible only to that shop's owner and the Super Admin.

## Who can see your information

- Your pro sees your name, your phone number, and your style card. Your pro never sees your email.
- The shop's owner can see clients of their own shop.
- Other clients never see your information.
- Pros at the same shop cannot see each other's clients unless the owner grants sharing of a style card.
- The Super Admin (the MyChair developer) can see everything, only for running and supporting the app. When the Super Admin views the app as another user, that session is recorded.

## Using more than one shop

If you use MyChair with several shops, each shop keeps its own record of you. A shop only sees your appointments and notes at that shop. Booking at one shop does not share your history with another.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords are stored as a one-way scrambled value (a hash), never as the password itself. Sessions use a signed token that expires. Owners never see or set a pro's password.

## Logs

MyChair keeps technical logs to find and fix problems. A log line records facts such as the page, the status, and how long it took, using IDs. Logs never contain passwords, tokens, email addresses, phone numbers, or the contents of a request. Logs are deleted after 14 days.

## Outside services

Service names and descriptions (not client information) are sent to an AI embedding provider so search can match meaning. Question text typed into the help assistant is sent to an AI provider to produce an answer. Photos and logos are kept in file storage, not in the database.

## Your choices

You can ask to correct or delete your information by contacting support. Deleting your account removes your profile; the shop may keep a record of past appointments as a business record.
