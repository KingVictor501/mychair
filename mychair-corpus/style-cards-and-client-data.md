# Style Cards and Client Data

A style card is a pro's private notes about one client and the service they usually get. This page explains what is on it and who can see it.

## What a style card holds

Preferences for the service, for example guard sizes and beard shape for a barber, nail shape and finish for a nail tech, or lash style and curl for a lash tech. It also holds free-text notes, photos, a visit history, and a time estimate for the client's usual service, such as "this fade takes 45 minutes."

## Who creates and edits it

Only the pro who serves the client creates and edits their own style cards. Owners can view cards in their shop but cannot edit them. Clients cannot see style cards.

## One card per pro

If a client sees two different pros, each pro keeps their own card. One pro cannot see the other's card unless the owner or Super Admin grants sharing between the two pros.

## The time estimate

When a client books, the pro's time estimate for that client is used as the appointment length. If there is no estimate, the service's default length is used. This keeps slots the right size.

## Visit history

When an appointment is marked Completed, it is added to the visit history on the client's card for that pro.

## Photos

Photos on a style card must be JPG, PNG, or WebP and no larger than 5 MB. SVG files are not allowed. Photos are kept in file storage, not in the database.

## What pros can see about a client

A pro sees only the client's name, phone number, and style card. They never see the client's email, and they cannot see clients of other pros. The no-show count is visible for their own clients.
