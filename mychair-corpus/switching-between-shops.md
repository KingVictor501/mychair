# Using More Than One Shop

One MyChair account can book at many shops. For example, you can book at your favorite nail shop and then switch to your hair salon from the same login and the same page.

## How it works

Your account is yours, not a shop's. When you book with a pro at a shop, you become a client of that shop. You can be a client of as many shops as you like with one name, one phone number, and one password.

## Switching shops

Use the shop switcher at the top of the page. It lists the shops you have booked with. Choose one and the page shows that shop's pros, services, prices, and open times. You do not need to log out or sign up again.

## Adding a new shop

To book at a shop you have not used before, open that shop's page or invite link and book as usual. The shop is added to your switcher after your first booking.

## Each shop keeps its own record

Every shop keeps a separate record of you. Appointments, no-show strikes, and style cards at one shop are never shown to another shop. Missing an appointment at your salon does not affect your nail shop.

## Your appointments across shops

The Appointments page can show bookings from all of your shops in one list, labeled by shop, so you can see your whole week at once. Cancelling follows the rules of the shop that holds the booking, including its cancellation window.

## Prices and times differ by shop

Prices, booking windows, cancellation windows, and time zones are set per shop. A time shown for one shop is in that shop's time zone.

## Leaving a shop

You can stop booking at a shop whenever you like by not booking again. To have that shop's record of you removed, contact support. See the privacy policy.
